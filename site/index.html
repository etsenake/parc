Before you go in

Questions at the gate.

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no subscription, no catch. Parc is open source under the AGPL, so every line is on GitHub.

Which Macs does it run on?

Apple Silicon Macs on macOS 13 Ventura or later. It updates itself, security fixes included.

Can I bring my Arc stuff?

Yes. Your Arc Spaces, pinned tabs and Favorites move in the way you left them. Bookmarks, passwords, history and extensions come along from Chrome, Safari, Firefox and the rest.

Do my extensions work?

Yes. Parc runs on its own build of Chromium, so extensions from the Chrome Web Store install the normal way.

Is anyone watching?

No analytics, no telemetry, no account. Your Notes and Easels live on your Mac, and so does your membership card.

Is this made by The Browser Company?

No. Parc is an independent project, built by one person who missed Arc. It isn’t affiliated with The Browser Company.
