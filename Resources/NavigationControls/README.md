# Navigation control artwork

`icon-browser-navigation-force-reload` is Parc's hard-refresh template image: a
circular arrow flowing into a doubled arrowhead, drawn on a 22 × 15-point canvas
with 2-point strokes. It has a vector `.pdf` plus `.png` and `@2x.png` renditions
at 22 × 15 and 44 × 30 pixels, rendered from the same geometry with 8×
supersampling.

`NavigationControlArtwork` combines the two bitmaps and vector at the 1× point
size and treats the result as a template. See [navigation](../../docs/navigation.md) for the
button's behavior.
