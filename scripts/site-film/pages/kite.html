How to fly a kite on a lazy afternoon

Updated today · 6 min read

Pick a wide-open hill, stand with your back to the wind and let out a few metres of line. A steady breeze of 10 to 25 km/h is perfect; any calmer and you’ll be running more than flying.

Diamond kites are the easiest place to start. Add a longer tail if it spins, and keep the bridle centred until it climbs on its own.
